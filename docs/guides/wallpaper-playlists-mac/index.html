macOS Wallpaper Playlists, Rotation & History (Free Guide)

Updated: November 13, 2026 • 6 min read • macOS 12+ (Intel & Apple Silicon) Free & Open Source

Quick Answer

How do you rotate curated wallpaper sets on a Mac without copying files into separate folders? Moonleaf lets you create named playlists that reference wallpapers in your library, pick In order or Random playback with per-list intervals (15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, or Daily), assign playlists to individual desktops, and switch lists right from the macOS menu bar.

Plus built-in recall & storage clarity: Moonleaf tracks up to your last 1,000 successful wallpaper applies in Recent and Timeline views, and separates saved library files from external references and clearable download cache in Settings → Storage.

Disclosure: Moonleaf is our free, MIT-licensed macOS wallpaper manager.

Why Named Playlists Beat Single-Folder Rotation

macOS System Settings can rotate pictures inside a single folder, which forces you to duplicate images across folders if you want separate "Work", "Minimal", and "Night" sets—and gives you no menu bar switch or apply history.

In Moonleaf, your Library holds the source wallpapers (or external links to folders you already keep), while Playlists act as lightweight collections pointing to them:

  • Zero file duplication: One wallpaper can belong to several playlists at once.
  • Safe editing: Removing an item from a playlist—or deleting the entire playlist—never deletes your wallpaper files.
  • Independent intervals & order: Each playlist keeps its own playback order (In order or Random) and schedule, even when global library shuffle is turned off.

Step 1: Create a Playlist & Multi-Select from Library

  1. Open Moonleaf and switch to the Playlists tab in the sidebar.
  2. Enter a Playlist name (for example, Focus or Ultrawide Landscapes) and click Create.
  3. Open the Library tab, search by filename (Search name or file) or filter by favorites/type, then click Select to pick multiple wallpapers at once and choose Add selected to add them to your playlist without interrupting what is currently on screen.

Back in Playlists, you can drag items to reorder them (or use the Up / Down buttons), inspect how many files in the list are available, and remove individual items anytime.

Step 2: Set Order, Interval & Desktop Target

Inside your selected playlist, configure how and where it rotates:

  • Order: Switch between In order (follows your custom item order) and Random.
  • Interval: Pick 15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, or Daily.
  • Desktop: Choose a target monitor × Space under Desktop and click Apply to desktop.

Want different playlists on different monitors or virtual Spaces? See our Per-Monitor & Space Wallpaper Guide.

Step 3: Switch Playlists & Shuffle from the Menu Bar

Once your playlists are ready, you don't need to keep the manager window open:

  • Click Moonleaf's menu bar icon and hover over Playlists to switch the active desktop to any playlist with one click.
  • Click Shuffle Now in the menu bar—or Shuffle all wallpapers in Overview—for an instant change.
  • Open Overview directly from the menu bar whenever you want to inspect all active displays and Spaces at a glance.

Step 4: Find Past Wallpapers in 1,000-Item Usage History

Ever seen a wallpaper rotate past and wished you knew its name? The History section in Overview records up to your latest 1,000 successful applies (both manual picks and automatic rotations, excluding startup restores):

  • Recent vs. Timeline: Browse a deduplicated list of recently used wallpapers or switch to Timeline to see changes grouped by day.
  • Search & Filters: Filter by filename, Display, Desktop, or Type (Still / Video).
  • One-Click Actions: Re-apply to the current desktop or display, toggle favorite, or open Details.
  • Non-Destructive Clear: Clicking Clear history removes recorded usage entries only—your wallpapers, favorites, and playlists stay untouched.

Step 5: Inspect Details, Esc Preview, Copy Image & Storage

Two quality-of-life tools make managing a large wallpaper library straightforward:

  • Unified Asset Details: Open Details on any item across Overview, Library, or Playlists to see its Format, Resolution, Size, Source (Library, External file, Discover, Wallhaven, or Pexels), Location, and playlist membership. Double-click the thumbnail (or click the hover preview icon) for a full-window preview—press Esc to close the preview—and click Copy image to copy still image data directly to your clipboard (or copy the file for videos/GIFs).
  • Transparent Storage Settings: Under Settings → Storage, Moonleaf separates Library files, External references (with Show paths so you can see linked files without copying them), Download cache (Clear download cache), and Clear memory thumbnails (clearly noted as in-memory only, not disk files).
Safe by design:

Clearing the download cache or memory thumbnails in Settings → Storage never deletes your saved library wallpapers or external folders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Moonleaf playlists copy my wallpaper files on disk?

No. Playlists store references to assets in your library rather than duplicating files. One wallpaper can belong to multiple playlists, and deleting a playlist or removing an item from a list keeps the original file untouched.

What rotation orders and intervals do Moonleaf playlists support?

Each playlist can play In order (reordered via drag-and-drop or Up/Down controls) or Random, and each playlist has its own rotation interval: 15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, or Daily. You can also assign different playlists to different desktops.

Can I switch wallpaper playlists from the macOS menu bar?

Yes. Click the Moonleaf icon in the menu bar and open the Playlists submenu to switch the current desktop to any named playlist, or click Shuffle Now for an immediate change.

How many wallpaper changes does Usage History keep?

Moonleaf keeps up to your last 1,000 successful wallpaper applies in Recent (deduplicated) and Timeline (day-by-day) views. Clearing history deletes recorded usage entries only—your wallpapers, favorites, and playlists remain intact.

Does clearing cache in Moonleaf delete my saved wallpapers?

No. Settings → Storage separates Library files, External references, Download cache, and Memory thumbnails. Clearing the download cache or memory thumbnails never deletes wallpapers in your library or external folders.
